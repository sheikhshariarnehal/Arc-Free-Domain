The dark metallic palette paired with electric sapphire blue accents (`#3b82f6`) gives the platform a distinct, high-craft brand identity.

#### Overall Impression
The interface feels extremely sleek, clean, and responsive. The unified search pill container is an ideal pattern for domain registration. A minor refinement to text contrast in suggestion pills will elevate the score to Excellent.

#### What's Working
1. **Unified Search Container**: Floating dark glass pill combining search icon, input, `.arc.bd` tag, and skeuomorphic button.
2. **Tactile Skeuomorphic Button System**: Flat-skeuomorphic design with inset light rim reflection and `active:scale(0.96)` click feel.
3. **Status Telemetry Pill**: Compact live edge DNS status badge with active pulse dot.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Text Contrast in Micro-Pills**: `text-slate-300` on dark glass background has slightly low contrast in certain lighting conditions.
  - **Why it matters**: Reduced legibility for quick suggestions on smaller screens.
  - **Fix**: Use `text-white` or `text-slate-200` with explicit opacity.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Alternative Suggestions when Subdomain is Taken**: When a name is unavailable, only the error is shown.
  - **Why it matters**: Increases drop-off when a requested name is taken.
  - **Fix**: Display 2-3 available alternate suffixes or prefixes.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`
